Team Schedule

10-person · 12-hour shifts · split-week rotation · starting Aug 2, 2026

How the schedule works

  • Two fixed halves — Front Half (Sun–Tue + alternating Wed) · Back Half (alternating Wed + Thu–Sat)
  • 12-hour shifts — Days 6am–6pm · Nights 6pm–6am
  • Weekly rotation — D1 → D2 → D3 → N1 → N2, repeating every 5 weeks
  • Wednesday alternates between halves each week
  • Result — Fixed days on/off · only your role changes week to week

Team setup

  • Use the → BH / ← FH button to move operators between halves
  • Position = starting role — slot 1 = D1, slot 5 = N2
  • Use the ▲ ▼ arrows to reorder within a half
  • Use ✕ to remove an operator — the rotation adjusts automatically
  • Switch to Individual, Crew, or Calendar view to explore the schedule
